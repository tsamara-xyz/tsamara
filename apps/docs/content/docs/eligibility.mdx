## Who may hold units

An investor holds units only after two things have happened onchain: the compliance authority
has written an investor record for their wallet, with a country code, and it has opened their
sukuk account, which starts frozen.

Registration is refused if the country is on the series' restricted list. The demo series is not
offered to residents of the United States or Indonesia. Indonesia is on the list because the
series settles in a stablecoin, and the rupiah is mandatory for domestic transactions there.

## How identity is checked

Verification runs through Sumsub, with an identity document and a liveness check. The widget in
the browser is only the interface. The decision is taken by the operator's API, which reads
Sumsub's own record of the verdict and the document's country, and registers the investor only
then.

<Callout type="warn">
The prototype uses Sumsub's sandbox, and the API refuses production credentials. The country is
the document's country, not proven residence. Sanctions and AML screening are not configured.
Identity checks and an allow-list are necessary for a real offering. They are not sufficient.
</Callout>

## What every transfer is checked against

On each transfer of units, the compliance program refuses unless:

- the recipient has an investor record for this series,
- the recipient's country is not restricted,
- the recipient's balance after the transfer is within the holding cap.

A revoked investor's account is frozen, so they can neither send nor receive.

## What the compliance authority can do

It can freeze an account. It can also move units without the holder's signature, for lost keys,
inheritance, or an order from a court or regulator. The library refuses to build such a transfer
without a reason and the authority that ordered it, and writes both onchain with the transfer.
The recipient must still be a registered investor within the holding cap.

Nothing onchain stops whoever holds that key from using it directly, so this control rests on
who holds the key. Holding it in a multisig is designed and not built
([issue 19](https://github.com/tsamara-xyz/tsamara/issues/19)).

## What is stored about you

The chain stores a wallet address and a two-letter country code per investor, and no other
personal data.
