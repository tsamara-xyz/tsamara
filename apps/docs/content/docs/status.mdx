## Proven

- **The whole lifecycle, on devnet.** Issue a series, approve investors, subscribe, transfer,
  pay a distribution, with assertions on the balances after each step.
- **The rules hold against attempts to break them.** Tests run the compiled program and try a
  transfer to an unregistered wallet, a forged recipient, a transfer over the cap, a revoked
  investor, and the hook called outside a transfer. Each is refused.
- **A real browser can do it.** A script drives Chrome through connect, fund, approval,
  subscribe, portfolio, transfer and a refused transfer, against devnet.
- **Identity verification end to end, in the sandbox.** A German test applicant is approved
  onchain; applicants from the United States and Indonesia are refused.
- **A distribution repeated is paid once.**

See [Testing](/docs/testing) for how to run each of these.

## Not true yet

- **No licence, no regulatory approval, no sharia certification.** No sharia board has reviewed
  the structure.
- **The token represents nothing.** There is no underlying sukuk, no custodian, no trust or
  wakalah arrangement, and no affiliation with any sovereign issuer.
- **Not audited.** The compliance program has had no professional security review.
- **Single keys.** The issuer and compliance authorities are single keys held by the operator's
  API, not a multisig.
- **The holding cap is not enforced by the program on a primary subscription**, only by the API.
- **Tangibility is published, not enforced.** Nothing updates the ratio or restricts transfers
  to par when it falls.
- **Sandbox identity checks only**, with no sanctions or AML screening.
- **Devnet only.**

The gaps are tracked as issues labelled
[`compliance`](https://github.com/tsamara-xyz/tsamara/labels/compliance) and
[`production`](https://github.com/tsamara-xyz/tsamara/labels/production), and mapped in
[Compliance mapping](/docs/compliance-mapping).
